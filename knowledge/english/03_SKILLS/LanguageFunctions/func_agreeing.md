# Language Function: Agreeing Fully and with Nuance

## 1. Repertorio Expresivo
- **Acuerdo Total:**
  - *"I completely agree with your assessment."*
  - *"You took the words right out of my mouth."*
  - *"That is precisely how I see the situation."*
- **Acuerdo Parcial o con Matices:**
  - *"I agree up to a point, provided that we consider the budget."*
  - *"That is certainly true in most cases, though exceptions exist."*
