# Language Function: Giving Advice and Recommendations

## 1. Repertorio Expresivo por Nivel
- **Nivel A2 (Consejos directos cotidianos y de salud):**
  - *"You should drink warm tea and go to bed early."*
  - *"You shouldn't look at your phone screen right before sleeping."*
  - *"It's a good idea to wear a warm coat today because it's cold."*
- **Nivel B1 (Consejos amigables y estructuras condicionales):**
  - *"If I were you, I would start reviewing vocabulary ten minutes every morning."*
  - *"You should make sure to back up all your project files on the cloud."*
  - *"You had better get some rest tonight instead of cramming until 3 AM."*
- **Nivel B2 (Recomendaciones profesionales estructuradas):**
  - *"It is highly advisable to establish a structured study timetable well in advance of exams."*
  - *"Under no circumstances should you submit written drafts without thorough peer editing."*
  - *"I strongly recommend exploring open-source repositories to familiarize yourself with standard coding conventions."*

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- En A2, enfatizar que *should* va seguido del infinitivo sin *to* (*You should go*, no *\*You should to go*).
- Contextualizar en A2 con situaciones de malestares cotidianos (dolor de cabeza, resfriado) y hábitos de estudio en la escuela.
