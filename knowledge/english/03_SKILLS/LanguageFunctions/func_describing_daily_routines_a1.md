# Describing Daily Routines (A1)

## 1. Repertorio Comunicativo
- **Enunciados en primera persona:**
  - *"I wake up at seven o'clock every morning."*
  - *"I brush my teeth and wash my face."*
  - *"I take the bus to school at eight o'clock."*
  - *"After school, I do my homework and play with my friends."*
- **Enunciados en tercera persona:**
  - *"My brother goes to bed at nine o'clock."*
  - *"She eats dinner with her family."*
- **Preguntas de rutina diaria:**
  - *"What time do you wake up? — At seven o'clock."*
  - *"When do you do your homework? — In the afternoon."*

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- Usar líneas de tiempo visuales o cómics con relojes analógicos para que los estudiantes relacionen imagen, hora y acción verbal.
- Vincular con conectores cronológicos básicos (*first, then, finally*).
