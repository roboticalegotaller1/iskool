# Language Function: Describing Past Experiences and Anecdotes

## 1. Repertorio Expresivo por Nivel
- **Nivel A2 (Experiencias cotidianas y viajes sencillos):**
  - *"Last weekend, I went camping with my family near a beautiful lake."*
  - *"We played board games and cooked dinner over a fire."*
  - *"Have you ever traveled by airplane? Yes, I have. I flew to Cancun last summer."*
- **Nivel B1 (Narrativa lineal y anécdotas personales):**
  - *"Last summer, our robotics club entered our very first regional competition."*
  - *"I have been learning guitar since I was twelve years old, and it has taught me discipline."*
  - *"While we were hiking through the national park, an unexpected storm rolled in."*
- **Nivel B2 (Narrativa vívida con tiempos complejos y reflexión personal):**
  - *"By the time we arrived at the youth summit, the keynote speaker had already begun addressing the plenary session."*
  - *"Having spent two weeks volunteering at the wildlife reserve, I gained an entirely new perspective on conservation ecology."*

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- En A2, trabajar la transición del Present Simple (rutinas fijas) al Past Simple (eventos concluidos con marcador temporal específico).
- Enfatizar la pronunciación de terminaciones *-ed* regulares (/t/, /d/, /ɪd/) y la familiarización con verbos irregulares de alta frecuencia (*go/went, see/saw, have/had*).
