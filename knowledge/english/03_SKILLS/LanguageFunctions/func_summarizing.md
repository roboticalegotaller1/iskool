# Language Function: Summarizing Key Arguments and Synthesizing Ideas

## 1. Repertorio Expresivo
- **Nivel B1 (Resumen secuencial de puntos principales):**
  - *"In brief, the author argues that excessive screen time impairs sleep quality."*
  - *"To sum up, our group recommends installing recycling stations across all school corridors."*
  - *"Overall, the experiment proved that ambient temperature affects battery performance."*
- **Nivel B2 (Síntesis conceptual y balance de perspectivas opuestas):**
  - *"In summary, while the initiative carries upfront capital expenditures, the anticipated educational dividends justify the investment."*
  - *"Synthesizing the two viewpoints, both agree on the urgency of decarbonization despite differing on the timeline."*
  - *"The essential takeaway from the study is that early bilingual immersion accelerates cognitive agility."*

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- Enseñar la nominalización para condensar oraciones largas (*"They investigated the problem" -> "Their investigation of the problem"*).
- Entrenar a los alumnos a no introducir opiniones personales nuevas en la fase de resumen de textos ajenos.
