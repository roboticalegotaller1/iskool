# Language Function: Asking for Clarification and Paraphrasing

## 1. Repertorio Expresivo por Nivel
- **Nivel A2 (Clarificación de números, palabras y mensajes sencillos):**
  - *"Could you say that again, please? Did you say page 14 or 40?"*
  - *"What does this word mean in our project?"*
  - *"Do you mean we have to work in pairs or groups of three?"*
  - *"I'm not sure what you mean. Can you show me an example?"*
- **Nivel B1 (Solicitudes directas de repetición o reformulación):**
  - *"Could you say that once more, please? I missed the final statistic."*
  - *"What exactly do you mean by 'passive solar design'?"*
  - *"If I understood correctly, the project deadline has been postponed to Friday?"*
- **Nivel B2 (Clarificación estratégica y verificación de implicaciones):**
  - *"Are you suggesting that automated translation will entirely replace human interpreters?"*
  - *"To ensure we are aligned, are you proposing that we split the research phase into two tracks?"*

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- En A2, enfatizar las preguntas de confirmación acústica (*"Did you say...?"*) y la entonación cortés con *please*.
- Fomentar la auto-corrección sin pánico escénico mediante fórmulas de reparación comunicativa (*"Let me rephrase that..."*).
