# Language Function: Justifying Claims and Giving Clear Reasons

## 1. Repertorio Expresivo por Nivel
- **Nivel A2 (Justificación directa y causa/efecto elemental):**
  - *"I like swimming because it helps me stay active and healthy."*
  - *"It was raining heavily, so we played indoors instead."*
  - *"The reason I arrived late is that my bus broke down."*
- **Nivel B1 (Justificaciones estructuradas con conectores de causa):**
  - *"The main reason for this decision is that our team needs more testing time."*
  - *"Since we have limited laboratory hours, we should prioritize simulation software."*
  - *"As the weather forecast predicted high winds, the outdoor trip was postponed."*
- **Nivel B2 (Atribución causal sofisticada y registro formal):**
  - *"This unexpected outcome is largely attributable to inconsistent testing protocols across trial runs."*
  - *"Owing to rapid advancements in renewable tech, production prices have decreased substantially."*

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- En A2, contrastar *because* (introduce la causa) con *so* (introduce el resultado o consecuencia).
- Evitar empezar oraciones con *Because* en la producción escrita formal temprana sin unir la cláusula principal.
