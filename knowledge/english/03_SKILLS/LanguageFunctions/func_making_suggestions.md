# Language Function: Making Suggestions and Proposing Actions

## 1. Repertorio Expresivo por Nivel
- **Nivel A2 (Sugerencias directas y fórmulas cotidianas):**
  - *"Shall we go to the cinema this afternoon?"*
  - *"Why don't we have a picnic in the park on Saturday?"*
  - *"Let's study English together after school."*
  - *"How about playing video games at my house?"*
- **Nivel B1 (Sugerencias colaborativas y justificaciones breves):**
  - *"Why don't we organize a workshop on digital privacy for our classmates?"*
  - *"How about creating an interactive infographic instead of a slide deck?"*
  - *"We could start by collecting survey feedback before making our final choice."*
- **Nivel B2 (Propuestas matizadas, hipótesis y cortesía diplomática):**
  - *"Perhaps it would be beneficial to pilot the initiative with a small test group first."*
  - *"I would suggest allocating distinct responsibilities before setting strict deadlines."*
  - *"One viable alternative might be to partner with local community organizations."*

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- En A2, asegurar el dominio del verbo base tras *Shall we*, *Why don't we* y *Let's*, contrastándolo con el gerundio tras *How about / What about* (*How about going...?*).
- En B1 y B2, practicar la modulación de entonación para sonar propositivo y evitar el error recurrente de transferir *\*suggest to do*.
