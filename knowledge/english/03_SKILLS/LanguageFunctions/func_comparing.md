# Language Function: Comparing Alternatives and Evaluating Contrasts

## 1. Repertorio Expresivo por Nivel
- **Nivel A2 (Comparaciones directas y superlativos cotidianos):**
  - *"A cheetah is faster than a lion, but the blue whale is the biggest animal in the world."*
  - *"My new smartphone is more expensive than my older tablet."*
  - *"Math is easier for me than history, but science is the most interesting subject."*
- **Nivel B1 (Comparaciones con grado y proporcionalidad simple):**
  - *"Online tutoring is far more flexible than traditional evening classes."*
  - *"This laptop model is not quite as durable as the desktop workstation."*
  - *"The more practice students get with coding, the easier problem solving becomes."*
- **Nivel B2 (Comparaciones sofisticadas con matices institucionales):**
  - *"While hybrid work models offer unprecedented autonomy, in-person labs facilitate deeper spontaneous collaboration."*
  - *"The environmental impact of synthetic textiles is substantially greater in comparison to organic alternatives."*

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- En A2, asegurar la distinción morfológica clara entre adjetivos cortos de una sílaba (*tall -> taller, big -> bigger*) y adjetivos largos de 2 o más sílabas (*interesting -> more interesting*).
- Prestar atención a las formas irregulares básicas: *good -> better -> the best*, *bad -> worse -> the worst*.
