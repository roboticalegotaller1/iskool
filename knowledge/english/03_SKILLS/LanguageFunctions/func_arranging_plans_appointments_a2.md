# Language Function: Arranging Plans, Times, and Appointments (A2)

## 1. Repertorio Expresivo
- **Proponer horarios y disponibilidad:**
  - *"Are you free on Saturday afternoon?"*
  - *"What time shall we meet? How about half past four?"*
  - *"Let's meet outside the cinema at 5:00."*
- **Aceptar y confirmar:**
  - *"That sounds great! See you there."*
  - *"Yes, I am free then. Let's do that."*
- **Rechazar cortésmente con excusa breve:**
  - *"I'm sorry, I can't. I'm visiting my grandparents on Saturday."*
  - *"I would love to, but I have basketball practice until 6:00."*

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- Enfatizar el uso de preposiciones de tiempo (*at 5:00, on Saturday, in the afternoon*).
- Practicar la entonación amistosa al declinar invitaciones para mantener la cortesía comunicativa.
