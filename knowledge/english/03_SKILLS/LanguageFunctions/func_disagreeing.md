# Language Function: Disagreeing Politely and Constructively

## 1. Repertorio Expresivo
- **Desacuerdo Diplomático Directo:**
  - *"I see what you mean, but from my perspective..."*
  - *"I am afraid I take a somewhat different view on this matter."*
- **Desacuerdo con Concesión Previa:**
  - *"While I acknowledge your point regarding cost, the long-term environmental gain is undeniable."*
  - *"That may sound convincing at first, yet recent findings challenge that assumption."*
