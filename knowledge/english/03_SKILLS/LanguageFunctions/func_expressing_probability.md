# Language Function: Expressing Probability, Likelihood and Certainty

## 1. Repertorio Expresivo
- **Nivel B1 (Probabilidad habitual y marcadores adyacentes):**
  - *"It will probably rain later this afternoon, so we should bring umbrellas."*
  - *"Our team is likely to qualify for the national stage if we win Friday's match."*
  - *"I doubt that the school board will approve the budget increase this term."*
- **Nivel B2 (Escalas precisas de probabilidad y certeza analítica):**
  - *"There is a strong likelihood that artificial intelligence will automate repetitive bookkeeping roles."*
  - *"Electric vehicle adoption is bound to surge once battery costs decline below the critical benchmark."*
  - *"In all probability, the atmospheric carbon reduction targets will necessitate stringent regulatory caps."*

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- Entrenar los tres patrones sintácticos de *likely*:
  1. *It is likely that + clause*
  2. *Subject + is/are likely to + infinitive*
  3. Adverbio: *will likely + verb*
- Comparar *bound to* (certeza inevitable), *likely to* (alta probabilidad), *unlikely to* (baja probabilidad).
