# Expressing Likes and Dislikes (A1)

## 1. Repertorio Comunicativo
- **Preferencias afirmativas:**
  - *"I like apples and bananas."*
  - *"I love swimming in summer."*
  - *"My favorite school subject is art."*
- **Preferencias negativas:**
  - *"I don't like broccoli."*
  - *"He doesn't like cold weather."*
- **Preguntas de indagación de gustos:**
  - *"Do you like pizza? — Yes, I do! / No, I don't."*
  - *"What is your favorite animal? — I like dolphins."*
- **Contraste inicial con conectores:**
  - *"I like dogs, but I don't like cats."*

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- Utilizar gráficos de barras colectivos en la pizarra para recopilar los gustos del salón (comidas favoritas, deportes).
- Enfatizar que tras "like" se utiliza sustantivo plural o incontable (*"I like apples"*, no *"I like apple"*).
