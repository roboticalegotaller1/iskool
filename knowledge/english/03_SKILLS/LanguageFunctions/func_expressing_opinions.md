# Language Function: Expressing Opinions and Personal Viewpoints

## 1. Repertorio Expresivo por Nivel
- **Nivel A2 (Opiniones sencillas sobre temas familiares):**
  - *"I think robotics class is the most exciting subject of the week."*
  - *"In my opinion, playing outside is better than watching TV all day."*
  - *"I don't think homework on weekends is necessary."*
  - *"What do you think about the new school library?"*
- **Nivel B1 (Opiniones fundamentadas y puntos de vista personales):**
  - *"In my opinion, students benefit from flexible learning hours because it promotes self-discipline."*
  - *"I believe that online resources make studying more accessible, though hands-on practice is still essential."*
  - *"From my point of view, social media has both significant advantages and clear drawbacks."*
- **Nivel B2 (Perspectivas matizadas y posturas críticas formales):**
  - *"As far as I am concerned, ethical oversight of automated systems is indispensable in educational technology."*
  - *"It seems to me that technological adoption must be paired with active digital wellbeing protocols."*
  - *"I am firmly convinced that experiential project work outweighs mere theoretical memorization."*

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- En A2, vincular de forma inmediata la expresión de opinión (*I think...*) con la causa explicativa (*...because...*).
- Practicar la entonación interrogativa al pedir la opinión de los compañeros en parejas (*What do you think?*).
