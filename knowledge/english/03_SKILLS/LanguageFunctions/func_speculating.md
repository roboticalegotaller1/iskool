# Language Function: Speculating and Formulating Hypotheses

## 1. Repertorio Expresivo
- **Nivel B1 (Especulación sobre situaciones presentes):**
  - *"They might be working late because the science fair deadline is tomorrow."*
  - *"It could be a system failure, or perhaps the server is undergoing maintenance."*
  - *"He looks as though he is preparing for an important presentation."*
- **Nivel B2 (Deducción modal y conjeturas en pasado/presente):**
  - *"The team must have conducted exhaustive usability trials before launching the application."*
  - *"Judging by the demographic shifts, this trend is likely to accelerate over the next decade."*
  - *"It can't have been an accidental error, given the stringent security protocols in place."*

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- Distinguir grados de certeza: certeza afirmativa (*must*), posibilidad (*might/could/may*), imposibilidad (*can't*).
- Formar modelos deductivos de pasado con *modal + have + past participle*.
