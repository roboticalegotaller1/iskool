# Lexical Domain: Clothing Items and Weather Conditions (A1)

## 1. Repertorio Léxico Esencial
- **Prendas de Vestir:** shirt, T-shirt, pants (trousers), shorts, jeans, dress, skirt, sweater (jumper), jacket, coat, shoes, sneakers (trainers), socks, boots, hat, cap, scarf, gloves, glasses.
- **Clima y Estaciones:** sunny, cloudy, rainy, windy, snowy, stormy, foggy, hot, warm, cold, spring, summer, autumn (fall), winter.

## 2. Colocaciones y Chunks Clave
- "What's the weather like today? It's sunny and hot."
- "Put on your coat!", "Take off your shoes!".
- "I'm wearing a blue T-shirt and black shorts."
- "When it rains, I need an umbrella and boots."

## 3. Pautas Pedagógicas
- Actividad de *"Weather Reporter"*: un alumno cada día presenta el pronóstico del clima del día y recomienda qué ropa vestir.
- Juego de vestir muñecos de papel magnéticos o tarjetas con atuendos acordes al clima dictado por el docente.
