# Lexical Domain: Family Members and Domestic Pets (Pre-A1)

## 1. Repertorio Léxico Esencial
- **Familia:** mother (mom), father (dad), parents, brother, sister, baby, grandmother (grandma), grandfather (grandpa), family.
- **Mascotas:** dog, puppy, cat, kitten, bird, fish, rabbit, turtle, hamster.

## 2. Expresiones y Chunks Clave
- "This is my mom / dad."
- "I have got a sister."
- "My dog is small and brown."
- "Who is this? It's my grandpa."

## 3. Pautas Pedagógicas
- Fomentar la inclusión y diversidad familiar en los dibujos y proyectos familiares.
- Evitar preguntas invasivas sobre la conformación del hogar; permitir incluir figuras significativas o mascotas.
