# Lexical Domain: Daily Routines, Clock Time and Days of the Week (A1)

## 1. Repertorio Léxico Esencial
- **Verbos de Rutina:** wake up, get up, wash my face, take a shower, brush my teeth, get dressed, comb my hair, have/eat breakfast, go to school, have lunch, do homework, play with friends, have dinner, go to bed, sleep.
- **Días de la Semana:** Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday, weekend, weekday.
- **Expresiones Temporales:** in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening, at night, at 7 o'clock, at half past eight.

## 2. Colocaciones y Chunks Clave
- "What time do you wake up? At 7:00."
- "On Mondays, I have English class."
- "Before school / after school."
- "It's time to sleep."

## 3. Pautas Pedagógicas
- Reloj analógico didáctico con manecillas móviles para práctica kinestésica en parejas.
- Cuadro de doble entrada para registrar el horario escolar semanal y compararlo con el horario de un estudiante de otro país.
