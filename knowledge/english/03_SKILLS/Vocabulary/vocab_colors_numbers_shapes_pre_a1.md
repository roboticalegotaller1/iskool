# Lexical Domain: Colors, Numbers (1-20) and Basic Shapes (Pre-A1)

## 1. Repertorio Léxico Esencial
- **Colores:** red, blue, green, yellow, orange, purple, pink, black, white, brown, grey.
- **Números Cardinales:** one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen, twenty.
- **Formas:** circle, square, triangle, rectangle, star, heart.

## 2. Chunks y Frases Modelo
- "What color is it? It's blue."
- "How many pencils? Three pencils."
- "Show me a red circle."

## 3. Pautas Pedagógicas
- Integrar objetos reales (*realia*) y bloques de colores manipulables.
- Practicar rondas de conteo rítmico asociadas a saltos o palmadas.
