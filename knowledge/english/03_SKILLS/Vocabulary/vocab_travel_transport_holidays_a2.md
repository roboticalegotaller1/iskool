# Vocabulary: Travel, Transport, and Holiday Destinations (A2)

## 1. Repertorio Léxico Fundamental A2
- **Medios de transporte:** *train, underground / subway, coach, helicopter, airplane, ferry, motorbike, bicycle, taxi*.
- **Viajes y equipaje:** *suitcase, backpack, passport, ticket, boarding pass, platform, gate, luggage, tourist information center*.
- **Alojamiento y vacaciones:** *hotel, campsite, tent, guest house, beach resort, sightseeing tour, postcard, souvenir*.
- **Colocaciones clave:** *go on holiday, travel by train, take a taxi, catch / miss the bus, arrive at the station, leave on time*.

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- Utilizar mapas temáticos interactivos de estaciones y aeropuertos para contextualizar el vocabulario en situaciones de viaje reales.
- Practicar la distinción entre preposiciones de transporte: *by car, by train, by plane* versus *on foot*.
