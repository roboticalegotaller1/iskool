# Vocabulary: Shopping, Stores, Services, and Prices (A2)

## 1. Repertorio Léxico Fundamental A2
- **Tipos de comercios:** *supermarket, department store, bookshop, clothes shop, baker's, butcher's, chemist's / pharmacy, shoe shop, kiosk*.
- **Dinero y pagos:** *money, cash, coins, notes / bills, credit card, price, receipt, change, sale, special offer, cheap, expensive*.
- **Ropa y tallas:** *try on, changing room, size (small / medium / large), fits well, too tight, too loose*.
- **Preguntas esenciales de compra:**
  - *"How much is this T-shirt? It's £ 15.99."*
  - *"Can I try this jacket on? Sure, the changing rooms are over there."*
  - *"Do you take credit cards or only cash?"*

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- Organizar un mercado escolar simulado (*School Flea Market*) donde los alumnos manejen dinero ficticio y recibos.
- Practicar la pronunciación de precios con decimales: *£ 4.50* se dice *"four pounds fifty"* (no *"four point fifty"*).
