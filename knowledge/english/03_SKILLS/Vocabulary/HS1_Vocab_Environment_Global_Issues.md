# Lexical Domain: Renewable Energy, Climate Action and Environmental Sustainability

## 1. Repertorio Léxico Delimitado (B1 → B2)
- **Términos Clave y Familias:**
  - `sustain` (v), `sustainability` (n), `sustainable` (adj).
  - `conserve` (v), `conservation` (n), `biodiversity` (n).
  - `deplete` (v), `depletion` (n), `fossil fuels` (n), `emissions` (n).
  - `renewable` (adj), `solar photovoltaic` (n), `geothermal` (adj).
- **Colocaciones Frecuentes:**
  - *Carbon footprint, transition towards clean energy, mitigate environmental damage, combat climate change, circular economy, ecological crisis*.
- **Phrasal Verbs Aplicables:**
  - *cut down on (waste), run out of (resources), phase out (plastics), wipe out (habitats)*.
