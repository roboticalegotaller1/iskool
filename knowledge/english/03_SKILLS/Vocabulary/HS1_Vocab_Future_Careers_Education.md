# Lexical Domain: STEAM Careers, Professional Skills and Higher Education

## 1. Repertorio Léxico Delimitado (B1 → B2)
- **Términos Clave y Familias:**
  - `curriculum` (n), `academic credential` (n), `degree` (n).
  - `internship` (n), `intern` (n/v), `mentor` (n/v), `mentorship` (n).
  - `competence` (n), `competent` (adj), `adaptability` (n), `resilience` (n).
  - `collaborate` (v), `collaboration` (n), `collaborative` (adj).
- **Colocaciones Profesionales:**
  - *Pursue a degree, gain hands-on experience, meet tight deadlines, problem-solving skills, leadership potential, career prospects, vocational pathway*.
- **Phrasal Verbs Aplicables:**
  - *take on (responsibilities), figure out (solutions), keep up with (trends), set up (projects)*.
