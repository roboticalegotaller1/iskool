# Lexical Domain: Hobbies, Sports and Free Time Activities (A1)

## 1. Repertorio Léxico Esencial
- **Deportes y Actividades Físicas:** play soccer (football), basketball, baseball, tennis, volleyball; ride a bike, skateboard, rollerblade; swim, run, jump rope.
- **Pasatiempos Artísticos y Creativos:** draw, paint, sing, dance, act, play the piano, play the guitar, listen to music, read books/comics.
- **Ocio Digital y de Mesa:** play video games, watch cartoons, play board games, build with blocks (Lego).

## 2. Colocaciones Sistemáticas
- **PLAY + deportes con pelota o juegos:** *play soccer, play basketball, play chess, play video games*.
- **PLAY THE + instrumentos musicales:** *play the guitar, play the piano, play the drums*.
- **GO + actividades terminadas en -ing:** *go swimming, go cycling, go fishing*.
- **DO + artes marciales / actividades individuales sin pelota:** *do gymnastics, do karate*.

## 3. Pautas Pedagógicas
- Actividad de mímica (*charades*): un estudiante actúa un deporte sin hablar y sus compañeros adivinan la frase con el colocador correcto (*"You play tennis!", "You go swimming!"*).
