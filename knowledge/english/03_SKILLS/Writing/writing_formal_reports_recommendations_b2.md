# Drafting Formal Technical Reports and Executive Recommendations (B2)

## 1. Descriptores Can-Do y Fundamento Pedagógico
- **Nivel CEFR:** B2 (Vantage / Independiente Avanzado).
- **Descriptor Can-Do:** El estudiante puede redactar un informe formal bien organizado que exponga hechos, evalúe una situación concreta y proponga recomendaciones viables para una autoridad o comité directivo.
- **Registro y Tono:** Registro formal, objetivo e impersonal. Se prioriza el uso de encabezados (*headings*), viñetas (*bullet points*) para recomendaciones y estructuras pasivas para mantener la neutralidad.

## 2. Convenciones Estructurales del Informe B2
- **Title:** *Report on Campus Energy Efficiency and Waste Reduction*
- **Introduction:**
  - *"The purpose of this report is to evaluate current energy consumption patterns across campus facilities and propose actionable efficiency measures."*
- **Key Findings:**
  - *"Interviews conducted with laboratory personnel revealed that 40% of heating systems remain active outside operating hours."*
  - *"According to the student survey, there is widespread dissatisfaction with the lack of battery recycling hubs."*
- **Evaluation / Analysis:**
  - *"While initial awareness campaigns produced momentary improvements, the lack of automated shutdown mechanisms undermines systemic savings."*
- **Recommendations:**
  - *"In light of these findings, it is recommended that the administration implement automated timer circuits."*
  - *"It is further suggested that student eco-delegates be appointed to oversee recycling compliance."*
