# Discourse Markers: Addition, Contrast, Concession and Logical Sequence

## 1. Descriptores Can-Do
- El estudiante articula textos y discursos orales cohesionados mediante marcadores lógicos que sobrepasan la coordinación básica (*and, but, so*).
- Domina las siguientes familias funcionales de transición:
  - **Contrast & Concession:** `However` (con coma inicial), `Although / Even though` (subordinante), `On the one hand... On the other hand...`.
  - **Addition & Reinforcement:** `In addition`, `Furthermore`, `What is more`, `Besides`.
  - **Consequence & Summary:** `Therefore`, `As a result`, `In conclusion`, `To sum up`.

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- **Regla de Puntuación:** Practicar explícitamente el uso de punto y coma o punto seguido con `However`:
  - *"The experiment was challenging. However, the students persevered"* o *"The experiment was challenging; however, the students persevered"*.
