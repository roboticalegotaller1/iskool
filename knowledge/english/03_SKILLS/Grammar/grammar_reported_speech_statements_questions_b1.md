# Reported Speech: Statements, Backshift, Pronoun Changes and Indirect Questions

## 1. Descriptores Can-Do
- El estudiante reproduce lo expresado por un tercero aplicando la concordancia temporal obligatoria (*Tense Backshift*):
  - *Present Simple → Past Simple* (*"I work hard" → He said he worked hard*).
  - *Present Continuous → Past Continuous* (*"We are studying" → They explained they were studying*).
  - *Will → Would* (*"I will help" → She promised she would help*).
- Adapta marcadores deícticos temporales y espaciales (*yesterday → the day before, tomorrow → the following day, here → there*).
- Transforma interrogativas a preguntas indirectas eliminando auxiliares de inversión (*"Do you speak English?" → She asked if I spoke English*).

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- **Error Crítico Frecuente:** Mantener la estructura de pregunta en el estilo indirecto (*"She asked where did I go"* ❌ → *"She asked where I had gone"* ✅).
