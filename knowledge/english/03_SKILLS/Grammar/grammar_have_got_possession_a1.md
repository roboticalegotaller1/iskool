# Possession with Have Got (A1)

## 1. Estructura Gramatical
- **Afirmativa:**
  - *I / You / We / They have got* (contraído: *'ve got*). Ej: *"I've got blue eyes."*
  - *He / She / It has got* (contraído: *'s got*). Ej: *"He's got a big dog."*
- **Negativa:**
  - *haven't got* / *hasn't got*. Ej: *"We haven't got homework today."*
- **Interrogativa y Respuestas Breves:**
  - *"Have you got a pencil? Yes, I have. / No, I haven't."*
  - *"Has she got a cat? Yes, she has. / No, she hasn't."*

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- Aclarar que *'s got* proviene de *has got* y no del verbo *be*.
- Práctica con tarjetas coleccionables o fotos de mascotas ficticias.
