# Advanced Passive Structures with Impersonal and Reporting Verbs

## 1. Fundamento Lingüístico y Pedagógico
- **Nivel CEFR:** B2 (Vantage / Independiente Avanzado).
- **Competencia Epistémica:** Uso de estructuras de pasiva con verbos de reporte (*say, believe, claim, report, consider, estimate, presume, allege*) para transmitir opiniones generalizadas, descubrimientos científicos preliminares o alegaciones sin comprometer la responsabilidad directa del autor.

## 2. Los Dos Patrones Sintácticos Canónicos

### Patrón 1: Estructura Impersonal con *It*
$$\text{It} + \text{Passive Reporting Verb} + \text{that} + \text{Clause}$$
- *"It is widely believed that artificial neural networks will transform healthcare diagnostics."*
- *"It was reported that the negotiations had reached an impasse late last night."*
- *"It has been estimated that over 30% of global marine species face habitat degradation."*

### Patrón 2: Estructura Personal con Infinitivo Simple o Perfecto
$$\text{Subject} + \text{Passive Reporting Verb} + \text{to-infinitive / to have + past participle}$$
- **Referencia a estado presente:**
  - *"The new compound is considered to be non-toxic under standard laboratory conditions."*
- **Referencia a hecho pasado anterior:**
  - *"The ancient city is believed to have flourished between the third and fifth centuries BCE."*
  - *"The suspects are alleged to have bypassed cyber defense perimeters intentionally."*

## 3. Matriz de Precisión Léxica
- *It is alleged that...* $\to$ Se afirma sin prueba concluyente (ámbito legal o periodístico).
- *It is estimated that...* $\to$ Cálculo o aproximación cuantitativa.
- *It is acknowledged that...* $\to$ Reconocimiento de un hecho o concesión a una postura rival.
