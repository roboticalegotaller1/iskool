# Mixed Conditionals: Connecting Past Hypotheses with Present Consequences

## 1. Fundamento Lingüístico y Pedagógico
- **Nivel CEFR:** B2 (Vantage / Independiente Avanzado).
- **Competencia Estructural:** Capacidad de romper la simetría temporal estándar de los condicionales clásicos (segundo y tercero) para vincular con exactitud causas pasadas no cumplidas con estados presentes reales, o premisas permanentes presentes con desenlaces históricos pasados.

## 2. Los Dos Patrones Canónicos Mixtos

### Patrón A: Condición en Pasado Irreal $\to$ Resultado en Presente
- **Estructura:** *If + Past Perfect, Subject + would / could / might + Base Form (now)*
- **Significado:** Un hecho no ocurrido en el pasado genera una consecuencia que perdura en el presente.
- **Ejemplos:**
  - *"If humanity had taken climate change seriously in the 1980s, our ecosystems would not be in such acute peril today."*
  - *"If I had accepted that university scholarship, I would be living in London right now."*

### Patrón B: Condición Permanente en Presente $\to$ Resultado en Pasado Irreal
- **Estructura:** *If + Past Simple, Subject + would / could / might + have + Past Participle*
- **Significado:** Un rasgo permanente de personalidad o verdad general impidió o provocó una acción en el pasado.
- **Ejemplos:**
  - *"If he were more open to constructive feedback, he wouldn't have alienated his entire research team during yesterday's meeting."*
  - *"If solar panels were not so expensive to manufacture initially, developing nations would have adopted them a decade earlier."*

## 3. Errores Comunes de Interferencia y Diagnóstico
- ❌ *"If I would have studied, I would be happy now."* $\to$ Inserción incorrecta de *would* en la cláusula condicional (*if-clause*).
- ✅ *"If I had studied, I would be happy now."*
