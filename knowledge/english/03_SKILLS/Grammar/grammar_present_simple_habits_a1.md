# Present Simple for Habits and Routines (A1)

## 1. Estructura Gramatical
- **Afirmativa:**
  - *I / You / We / They + Verbo Base* (*"I eat breakfast"*, *"They play soccer"*).
  - *He / She / It + Verbo + -s / -es* (*"She walks"*, *"He catches"*, *"It runs"*).
- **Negativa:**
  - *I / You / We / They + don't + Verbo Base*.
  - *He / She / It + doesn't + Verbo Base*.
- **Interrogativa:**
  - *"Do you play basketball? Yes, I do. / No, I don't."*
  - *"Does he live here? Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't."*

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- Prestar atención explícita al error fosilizado de omisión de la *-s* en tercera persona singular mediante la regla del *"Super S"* en primaria.
- Conectar con cronogramas de clases y rutinas familiares reales.
