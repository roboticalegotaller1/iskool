# Present Perfect Continuous: Ongoing Activities, Duration and Visible Present Results

## 1. Descriptores Can-Do
- El estudiante construye enunciados con `have/has been + verb-ing` para destacar la continuidad o duración ininterrumpida de una acción iniciada en el pasado que sigue vigente o acaba de finalizar con secuelas visibles.
- Responde con precisión a preguntas que indagan duración: *"How long have you been studying robotics?" → "I have been studying robotics for eighteen months"*.
- Aplica la restricción de verbos de estado (*Stative Verbs*): utiliza Present Perfect Simple para verbos como `know, understand, own, be` (*"I have known him for years"*, no *"have been knowing"*).

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- **Matriz de Contraste:**
  - *Result / How much / How many:* Present Perfect Simple (*"She has written three essays this week"*).
  - *Process / Duration / How long:* Present Perfect Continuous (*"She has been writing all morning"*).
