# Cleft Sentences (Wh- and It- Clefts) for Thematic Focus and Emphasis

## 1. Fundamento Lingüístico y Pedagógico
- **Nivel CEFR:** B2 (Vantage / Independiente Avanzado).
- **Competencia Pragmática y Sintáctica:** La escisión oracional (*clefting*) permite dividir una proposición simple en dos partes para dotar a un constituyente de prominencia informativa máxima (*foco temático*).

## 2. Tipología Canónica

### A. It-Clefts (Oraciones Hendidas con It)
- **Fórmula:** *It + be (en tiempo correspondiente) + elemento enfatizado + that / who + resto de la oración*
- **Ejemplos:**
  - Base: *"Dr. Thorne discovered the genetic anomaly in 2021."*
  - Sujeto focalizado: *"It was Dr. Thorne who discovered the genetic anomaly in 2021."*
  - Fecha focalizada: *"It was in 2021 that Dr. Thorne discovered the anomaly."*

### B. Wh-Clefts / Pseudo-Clefts (Oraciones Hendidas con Wh-)
- **Fórmula:** *What-clause + be + elemento focalizado*
- **Ejemplos:**
  - Base: *"We need a unified international regulatory framework."*
  - Cleft: *"What we urgently need is a unified international regulatory framework."*
  - Con verbos de acción: *"What the research team did was publish their dataset open-source."*

### C. Variaciones Especializadas B2
- **The reason why... is that...:** *"The reason why this pilot initiative succeeded is that it engaged the local community from day one."*
- **All that...:** *"All that is required to participate is verified student registration."*
- **It was not until... that...:** *"It was not until the subsequent peer review that the flaw became apparent."*
