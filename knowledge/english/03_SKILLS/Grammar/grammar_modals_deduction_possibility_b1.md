# Modal Verbs of Deduction and Possibility in the Present: Must, Might, May, Could, Can't

## 1. Descriptores Can-Do
- El estudiante formula inferencias lógicas en presente graduando su grado de certeza según la evidencia disponible:
  - `Must + verb`: Certeza casi total afirmativa (*"Her lights are on, so she must be home"*).
  - `Can't + verb`: Certeza casi total negativa (*"He can't be at work; it's Sunday and the office is closed"*).
  - `Might / May / Could + verb`: Posibilidad abierta o conjetura (*"It could rain later, but the forecast is uncertain"*).
- Evita el error frecuente de utilizar *"mustn't"* para deducción negativa (se debe emplear exclusivamente `can't`).

## 2. Pautas Pedagógicas
- **Escala de Certeza Visual:**
  - 100% Certeza: *Must*
  - 50% Posibilidad: *May / Might / Could*
  - 0% Imposibilidad: *Can't*
