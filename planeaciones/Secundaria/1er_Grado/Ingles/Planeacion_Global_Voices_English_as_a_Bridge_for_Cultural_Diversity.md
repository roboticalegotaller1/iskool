# Global Voices: English as a Bridge for Cultural Diversity

> [!INFO] **Ficha Técnica NEM 2022**
> - **Docente Titular:** Prof. Israel López Ángeles
> - **Nivel / Fase:** Secundaria • Fase 6 (1er Grado)
> - **Campo Formativo:** Lenguajes
> - **Asignatura:** Ingles
> - **Duración Estimada:** 2 sesiones de 50 minutos (Total: 100 min)
> - **Ejes Articuladores:** Interculturalidad Crítica • Pensamiento Crítico

---

## 🎯 I. Proceso de Desarrollo de Aprendizaje (PDA)

> **Fase 6 (1º Secundaria) - Hace uso del alfabeto, los números y las expresiones básicas en inglés para recuperar información sobre la diversidad lingüística y cultural en países anglófonos y no anglófonos, presentándola mediante fichas informativas.**

---

## ❓ II. Preguntas Detonadoras para el Salón (Apertura y Conflicto Cognitivo)

1. **Why is English considered a global language (lingua franca) today?**

2. **How do people greet and express their cultural traditions in different English-speaking countries (UK, USA, Australia, South Africa, India)?**

3. **How can we use English to share Mexican traditions with the rest of the world?**

---

## ⏱️ III. Secuencia Didáctica (Dosificación en Bloques de 50 minutos)

### 📌 Inicio (10 minutos)
⏱️ SESIÓN 1 (50 min) — INICIO (10 min):
1. Warm-up: Listening to different English accents around the world (BBC, American, Australian, Jamaican, Indian).
2. Driving Question: "Did you know that there are more non-native English speakers in the world than native speakers? Why?".
3. Brainstorming: Vocabulary related to countries, nationalities, languages, and traditional greetings.

### 🔬 Desarrollo (30 minutos)
⏱️ SESIÓN 1 — DESARROLLO (30 min):
1. Pair Work Reading: Short infographic about 4 diverse countries where English is an official language.
2. Vocabulary & Grammar Practice: Simple present tense ("They speak...", "In Kenya, people greet by...").
3. Fact Sheet Creation: In pairs, design a bilingual cultural profile card of an assigned country including flags, languages spoken, greetings, and typical cultural traditions.

### 💡 Cierre (10 minutos)
⏱️ SESIÓN 1 — CIERRE (10 min):
1. Gallery Walk: Students display their cultural profile cards on the classroom walls.
2. Quick Peer Quiz: Asking and answering 3 questions in English to classmates.
3. Exit Ticket: "One new fact I learned in English today about world cultures".

---

## 📋 IV. Evaluación Formativa y Rúbrica Analítica

📋 FORMATIVE RUBRIC (3 Criteria):
• Criterion 1 - Language Accuracy (Outstanding: Correct use of simple present, subject-verb agreement and vocabulary | Satisfactory: Minor spelling errors with clear message | Developing: Incomplete sentences).
• Criterion 2 - Cultural Content (Outstanding: Rich and accurate cultural data | Satisfactory: Basic information | Developing: Missing data).
• Instrument: Checklist & Peer Assessment.
